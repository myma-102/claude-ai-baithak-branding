Five calm, minimal color options

These replace the earlier G to K, which were too vibrant. Every option here is low in saturation: soft neutrals, one muted accent, and a darker variation for emphasis. Your orange is softened to apricot or clay and used once per post. The lettering continues from the first board (A to F), so these are L to P.

How to keep it calm

Mostly neutral

Aim for about 80% neutral colors: the base, the card color and the text. The accent colors fill the rest.

One accent per post

Pick a single accent for each post. The signature color goes on one highlight block, button or shape, with dark text on top.

Dark for emphasis

Use the dark variation for events, quotes and announcements, roughly one post in three. The rest stay on the light base.

Let the photos lead

With quiet colors, the people and headlines carry the post. Keep photos large and give text room around it.

My pick

Use L · Warm Neutral as the main look. It is the calmest option that still feels like AI Baithak, because the apricot is a softer version of your orange and the charcoal stands in for your black.

If the team wants the most minimal feed, choose P · Mono Ink. For a cooler, more professional tone choose N · Dusty Blue.
