Using the covers

New posts first

Instagram does not let you swap the image on a published photo post. Use a cover as the first slide of a new carousel, with the poster or photos as the next slides. On a reel you can change the cover after posting.

Partner posts

Send the partner your cover and ask them to use it as slide one when they co-post. Posts published from their account can only be changed by them.

Keep the color order

Covers run black, white, orange down the diagonal, so no two neighbors match. A new post takes the color that differs from the cover above and the cover to its left.

Logos stay on the cover

For any collab, put the partner's logo next to yours on the cover. Use the black bar on orange and white covers, and the white bar on black ones.
