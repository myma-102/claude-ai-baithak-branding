Five lighter color options

Each option is light first. Most posts sit on a light base, with a dark variation for emphasis and one bold accent post. Orange stays the signature, except in K, where terracotta takes its place. The lettering continues from the first board (A to F), so these are G to K.

Each option shows six sample posts (three of them with people and the robot from your workshop post), story highlight covers and a nine-post feed. Click any color to copy its hex code.

Side by side

How the light and dark variations work

Light is the home base

Most posts use the light base, so the profile feels bright. The dark variation is for strong moments like events, quotes and announcements.

Accent base now and then

Use the bold accent base for about one post in six, for spotlights and big calls to action.

The signature color is a highlight block

On light backgrounds, orange text is hard to read. The signature color goes on highlight blocks, buttons and shapes, with dark text on top.

Mix the grid

Aim for light, dark and accent in every row of three. The feed preview in each option shows the pattern.

My pick

Use G · Sky & Sun as the main look. Orange and blue are opposites, so your signature stands out. Navy replaces black for the dark posts, and the blue reads as clear and trustworthy, which suits an AI community.

For a warmer, community feel choose H · Blush & Berry. For something different from most tech pages choose K · Clay & Olive, but check the terracotta next to your logo first.

This is a recommendation. The final choice is the team's.
