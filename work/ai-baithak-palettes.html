Six color options for AI Baithak

Options A to E keep orange as the signature and add one or two extra colors, so the feed has more range without turning messy. Option F drops orange and uses only four flat colors: black, mustard, white and cream. All of them use the layouts you already post.

Each option shows six sample posts (three of them with people and the robot from your workshop post), story highlight covers and a nine-post feed. Click any color to copy its hex code.

How to use any of them

Base 60% Text + cards 25%

Base 60%, text and cards 25%, orange 10%, extra accents 5%.

Orange leads

Orange is for the key word in a headline, dates, buttons and the logo mark. Accent colors go on labels, icons and one or two words, never on whole blocks of text. In option F, mustard does the job of orange.

One color per topic

Events use orange, team posts use accent 1, learning posts use accent 2. Story covers follow the same rule, so the profile reads as a system and not a random mix.

Mix the grid in threes

In each row of three, post one base-color post, one card or accent post and one bold post. The feed preview in each option shows this rhythm.

Check the text

Body text is only white or ink. Small orange text on a light background is hard to read, so keep orange for large words and shapes there.

My pick

Use B · Deep Teal as the main look and D · Cream Studio for roughly one post in four. B keeps your dark brand as it is, and orange against teal gives the strongest contrast. D answers the point that the feed does not always have to be dark.

If the team wants the smallest change from today, choose A · Ember Violet. Keep E · Orange Pop for big announcements only. If the team wants the strictest system, F · Mustard & Cream is the easiest to keep consistent because it has only four colors, though it has less range than the others.
