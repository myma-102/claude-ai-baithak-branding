Using the covers

New posts first

Instagram does not let you swap the image on a published photo post. Use a cover tile as the first slide of a new carousel, with the poster or photos as the next slides. On a reel you can change the cover after posting.

Partner posts

Send the partner your cover tile and ask them to use it as slide one when they co-post. Posts published from their account can only be changed by them.

Keep the color order

Tiles run black, white, orange down the diagonal, so no two neighbors match. A new post takes the color that differs from the tile above and the tile to its left.

Highlights match too

The highlight covers use the same three colors in the same order, so the top of the profile and the grid read as one set.
